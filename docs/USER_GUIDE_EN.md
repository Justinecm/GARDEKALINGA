# GARDEKALINGA user guide

Open the garden's web address on your phone. There is nothing to install.
You can add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

## Sign in
Type the username and password the garden manager gave you. Choose **English** or **Filipino**
the first time; you can change it later in the menu (☰).

## The Garden screen
- **The picture** shows the three plots. Dark soil is wet; pale soil is dry. Plants droop when a plot is too dry.
  A grey striped plot means its sensor has a problem. Tap a plot to see its reading.
- **The big sentence** tells you what you need to know: *Moist enough*, *Getting dry*, *One plot is too dry*,
  or *Watering, 2 min left*.
- **The numbers** are the average and each plot's soil moisture (%).
- **The line under them** says what the controller decided at the last check, and why.

| Colour | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Moist enough | No watering needed |
| Yellow | Getting dry | Close to the limit; watering may start at the next check |
| Orange | Below minimum | One plot is too dry; watering starts unless rain is coming |
| Red | Sensor problem / Controller offline | Something needs a person to check |

## Watering by hand
1. Tap **Water now**.
2. **Check that the drum has water.** Choose 1, 2 or 3 minutes. Tap **Start watering**.
3. The screen says *Sending to the controller…*, then *Watering* when the valve is really open.
4. Tap **Stop watering** at any time.

You cannot start when the controller is offline, when watering is already running, or while every
sensor has a problem (the fixed 6 AM / 4 PM schedule is running). If the soil is still soaking from
the last watering, the app asks you first.

## When the controller is offline
The controller keeps watering on its own and saves its readings. Check that the Wi-Fi router in the
house is on. The readings appear when the connection comes back.

## Other screens
- **History**: soil moisture over 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. Tap **Show as table** for the numbers.
- **Forecast**: rain chance and amount for the next 24 hours, the rain pause, and the next-hour estimate.
- **Records**: every watering, power change and decision.
- **Help**: the colours and words, and what to do.

Managers also have **Settings**, **Users** and **Reports**. Community leaders can view everything and download reports, but cannot water.
